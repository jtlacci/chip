Privacy

CHIP is a private agent app for a small group of people who an owner invites.

What CHIP reads

When you sign in with Google, CHIP gets only your name and email address. It never gets access to your own mail or calendar.

CHIP has its own Gmail account and Google Calendar. CHIP reads that account to make its feed and to answer in chat. It holds only what people send to CHIP or invite it to.

What CHIP does with it

CHIP shows this information to the people in its space. It hides one-time codes and sign-in links from the feed and the chats. CHIP sends an email or changes a calendar event only after an owner allows that action.

Where it is kept

CHIP's records are on CHIP's own server and its backups. They are not sold or shared with anyone else.

Removing your data

Ask an owner to remove you. Owners can remove anyone except the admin.
